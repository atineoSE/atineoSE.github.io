Privacy Policy

LinkedIn application · Last updated: 14 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how the LinkedIn application operated by Adrian Tineo (“the App”, “I”, “me”) handles your information when you connect it to your LinkedIn account. The App is a small, independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by LinkedIn Corporation. This policy applies only to the App. It does not cover adriantineo.com or any other website or service I operate.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Adrian Tineo, based in Málaga, Spain. You can contact me at me@adriantineo.com.

2. What data the App accesses

The App only accesses data that you explicitly authorize on LinkedIn's consent screen. Depending on the permissions you grant, this can include:

  • Basic profile information: your name, LinkedIn member ID, and profile photo.
  • The primary email address of your LinkedIn account.
  • The ability to create posts on your behalf, if you grant that permission. The App only posts content that you have reviewed and requested.

The App does not access your connections, messages, or any other LinkedIn data beyond the permissions shown on the consent screen.

3. How the data is used

Your data is used only to provide the App's functionality to you: to identify you when you sign in and to perform the actions you request. The App does not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the App.

4. Storage and retention

The App stores only the minimum data needed to work, such as the access token issued by LinkedIn. Access tokens are stored securely and expire according to LinkedIn's policies. Data is kept only for as long as you use the App and is deleted when you revoke access or ask for deletion.

5. Sharing

I do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. Data is only sent to LinkedIn through its official API in order to carry out the actions you request, or disclosed if required by law.

6. Legal basis

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the App processes your data based on your consent, which you give when you authorize the App on LinkedIn. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your data, and to object to its processing. To exercise any of these rights, email me at me@adriantineo.com. You also have the right to file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) or your local data protection authority.

8. Revoking access

You can revoke the App's access at any time from your LinkedIn account under Settings → Data privacy → Permitted services. After you revoke access, the App can no longer read or act on your LinkedIn data.

9. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.
